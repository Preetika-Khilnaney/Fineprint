MASTER VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Vendor Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Northwind Cloud Systems Pvt. Ltd. ("Vendor") and Brightline Retail Ltd. ("Customer").

1. Definitions
1.1 "Services" means the cloud hosting, maintenance and support services described in Schedule A.
1.2 "Customer Data" means all data, including personal data of Customer's shoppers, uploaded by Customer to the Services.

2. Term and Renewal
2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of two (2) years.
2.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. Payment Terms
3.1 Customer shall pay Vendor annual Fees of $240,000, invoiced quarterly in advance.
3.2 All invoices are payable Net 30 from the invoice date.
3.3 Undisputed late payments shall accrue interest of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.
3.4 Vendor may increase the Fees once per year, on at least sixty (60) days' written notice, by no more than five percent (5%).
3.5 Customer shall reimburse only pre-approved, documented out-of-pocket expenses.

4. Services and Service Levels
4.1 Vendor shall make the Services available at least 99.9% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
4.2 If availability falls below this level, Customer will receive service credits of up to 10% of the monthly Fees.

5. Confidentiality
5.1 Each party shall hold the other party's Confidential Information in confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party.
5.2 The obligations in this Section 5 survive for five (5) years after termination.

6. Intellectual Property
6.1 Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. Customer owns all Customer Data and all deliverables created specifically for Customer under this Agreement.
6.2 Customer grants Vendor a limited, non-exclusive licence to use Customer Data solely to provide the Services during the term.

7. Warranties
7.1 THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

8. Limitation of Liability
8.1 Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential or special damages, except for breach of confidentiality or data protection obligations.
8.2 Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed two times (2x) the Fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

9. Indemnification
9.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent caused by its own negligence, wilful misconduct or material breach of this Agreement.

10. Termination
10.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice to the other party.
10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice.
10.3 Upon termination, Customer shall pay only for Services delivered up to the effective date of termination.

11. Non-Solicitation
11.1 For twelve (12) months after termination, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was directly involved in the Services.

12. Dispute Resolution
12.1 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration seated in Wilmington, Delaware, and Customer waives any right to a jury trial or class action.

13. Governing Law
13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

14. Data Protection
14.1 Vendor shall process personal data only on Customer's documented instructions and maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
14.2 Vendor shall notify Customer of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours.

15. Force Majeure
15.1 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party promptly.

16. General
16.1 This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorised representatives of both parties.
16.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
16.3 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.
